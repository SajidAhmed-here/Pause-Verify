# Pause & Verify — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

Pause & Verify is a Chrome extension that checks Facebook images and videos for signs of AI generation before you share them.

## What the extension processes

- **Images and video frames from Facebook posts.** These are analysed **on your own device** by a model built into the extension. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else, and they are not saved.
- **Post caption, author name, and your reaction or share.** These are stored **only on your device** (Chrome local storage) so the extension's dashboard can show your recent checks. They are deleted automatically after 7 days, and you can delete them at any time from the popup (Settings → Clear All Data).
- **Your settings.** For example theme, thresholds and screen-time limit. These are stored only on your device.

## What we collect

Nothing. The extension has no account, no analytics, no tracking, and no server. We do not sell, share or transfer any user data.

## Network requests

- The extension runs on facebook.com pages and reads images from Facebook's image servers (fbcdn.net) to analyse them on your device.
- If the detection model is not bundled with your version of the extension, it is downloaded **once** from huggingface.co and kept in the browser cache. No personal data is sent in that request.

## Limited use

Use of information received from Chrome APIs complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
